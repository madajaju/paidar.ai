Understand Your AI Readiness Before You Scale

Assessments create the baseline required for confident investment decisions, practical execution plans, and accountable AI adoption across your organization.

Why Start Here

Why Start With an Assessment

  • No Baseline, No Progress

    Without a current-state baseline, outcomes are hard to prioritize and harder to measure.

  • Fragmented Adoption Wastes Investment

    Uncoordinated initiatives lead to duplicated effort, uneven capability, and weak ROI.

  • Governance and Workflow Gaps Increase Risk

    Readiness assessments expose policy, process, and workflow breakdowns before they become costly.

Assessment Types

Assessment Options by Scope

Individual Assessment

Who it is for: Professionals and practitioners building AI capability in their role.

What it measures: Applied skill, workflow readiness, and AI literacy for day-to-day execution.

What you receive: Readiness scorecard, strengths/gap profile, and prioritized development actions.

Likely next step: Targeted workshop enrollment or role-specific enablement plan.

Team Assessment

Who it is for: Functional and cross-functional teams adopting AI in delivery workflows.

What it measures: Team alignment, process fit, collaboration maturity, and operational blockers.

What you receive: Team capability benchmark, risk summary, and 30/60/90-day action plan.

Likely next step: Facilitated team workshop and execution cadence support.

Organizational / Executive Assessment

Who it is for: Executive leaders and transformation owners managing enterprise AI adoption.

What it measures: Strategy alignment, governance posture, operating model, and scaling readiness.

What you receive: Executive briefing pack, enterprise readiness heatmap, and phased roadmap.

Likely next step: Advisory engagement with governance and implementation planning.

Process

What the Assessment Process Looks Like

1. Intake

Define business context, goals, constraints, and priority outcomes.

2. Evaluation

Collect readiness data across people, workflows, governance, and execution capability.

3. Review

Present findings, risks, and maturity profile with clear decision points.

4. Roadmap

Deliver a practical next-step roadmap aligned to your timeline and operating reality.
