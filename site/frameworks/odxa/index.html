ODXA: Open Digital Transformation Architecture

ODXA is an open architecture approach for digital transformation across broader organizations and ecosystems.

It helps leaders align business and technology without forcing the work into a closed or vendor-specific model.

What is ODXA?

ODXA is a practical reference for designing transformation work that can operate across teams, partners, and service boundaries.

Why open architecture matters

Open architecture supports interoperability, reuse, and clearer decision-making when transformation spans multiple systems or organizations.

Business and technology alignment

ODXA helps leaders connect priorities, capabilities, and controls so transformation work stays executable.

Relationship to GDXA and GEAR

GEAR provides the government reference, GDXA provides the modernization lens, and ODXA expands the idea to broader ecosystems.

Next step

Use workshops or advisory when you need to turn architecture into an execution plan that teams can actually run.
