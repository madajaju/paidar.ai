Methodology

The AAOS Delivery Model

All engagements follow our six-phase AI-Augmented Operating System (AAOS) methodology to ensure sustainable organizational change.

1. Diagnose

Assess current state, identify capability gaps, and establish a clear maturity baseline.

2. Activate Talent

Engage participants and build foundational literacy and personal workflow discipline.

3. Apply Controls

Implement governance structures, validation mechanisms, and reviewable accountability.

4. Execute

Apply the operating model within real-world business workflows and production tasks.

5. Measure

Evaluate adoption, output quality, and decision reliability using structured metrics.

6. Scale

Extend governance and capability across additional teams, functions, and domains.
