AAOS

AI-Augmented Operating System (AAOS) Framework

AI increases speed; without controls it also scales error. AAOS gives leaders and teams a practical reliability system from decision through execution.

Built for executives, transformation leaders, functional heads, team leaders, and practitioners.

Six-Stage System

Diagnose to Scale

1. Diagnose

Map current posture, risks, workflow friction, and readiness gaps.

Outcome: Teams share a clear baseline and priority problem set.

2. Activate

Select high-consequence workflows and align stakeholders on initial scope.

Outcome: Priority workflows have owners, sequence, and decision criteria.

3. Controls

Define validation, approvals, escalation paths, and traceability expectations.

Outcome: Reliability controls are explicit before execution starts.

4. Execute

Run AI-assisted workflows with role clarity and consequence-matched checks.

Outcome: Work moves faster with accountable human oversight.

5. Measure

Track trust signals and operating outcomes, not just usage.

Outcome: Leaders can see reliability drift early and act quickly.

6. Scale

Standardize what works across teams with reusable artifacts and governance continuity.

Outcome: Capability expands without compounding hidden risk.

Integrity Packet

Core Transfer Artifact

The Integrity Packet is the handoff object that preserves reliability between people, teams, and workflow stages.

  • Outcome: The decision, deliverable, or action expected.
  • Visible Assumptions: Explicit assumptions and constraints.
  • Evidence/Traceability: Sources, prompts, data lineage, and rationale.
  • Validation Status: What checks passed, failed, or remain open.
  • Ownership: Prepared By, Validated By, Approved By.
  • Risk: Consequence level, unresolved exposure, and escalation trigger.

Reliability vs Adoption

Why Adoption Metrics Are Not Enough

Adoption tells you AI is being used. Reliability tells you whether outcomes are safe, defensible, and repeatable under consequence.

  • High usage can coexist with hidden rework and policy exposure.
  • Fast output without validation can increase escalation load later.
  • AAOS treats reliability as an operating requirement, not a reporting afterthought.

Operational Trust Signals

Metrics That Show Real Control

Validation pass rate

Percent of outputs meeting consequence-matched checks on first review.

Correction load

Rework volume required to make outputs decision-ready or production-safe.

Approval clarity

Rate of artifacts with unambiguous signoff responsibility.

Escalation timeliness

Time from detected risk to routed escalation and response.

Role Clarity

Accountability by Consequence Level

Prepared By

Builds the packet, documents assumptions, and assembles evidence.

Validated By

Performs consequence-matched validation before release or decision use.

Approved By

Authorizes action and accepts residual risk within defined thresholds.

  • Day 60: Controls + Execute running in one high-consequence workflow.
  • Day 90: Measure + Scale in motion with dashboard and governance cadence.

Expected artifacts: posture assessment, Integrity Packet set, control map, trust dashboard, scale playbook.
