Asset description

Work Classification and Consequence Worksheet

The worksheet guides a team through a bounded workflow, assesses its consequence dimensions, assigns a Low, Medium, or High consequence level, and translates that level into a control posture.

The need

Why use this asset?

Teams can approve an AI use case because it appears routine while overlooking sensitive data, difficult to reverse errors, affected people, or unclear decision authority.

Good uses for this asset

  • Reviewing a proposed AI use case before deployment
  • Determining the required human review and approval depth
  • Documenting why a workflow has a particular consequence classification
  • Identifying stop conditions and reassessment triggers

Example uses

Supplier proposal comparison
A team compares supplier proposals against an approved rubric. Because the result affects money and supplier selection, the worksheet supports a Medium consequence classification and requires a named validator and buyer approval.

Eligibility or service recommendation
A workflow uses sensitive records to recommend an action affecting a person’s access or service. The worksheet identifies High consequence, restricted AI use, independent validation, explicit approval, and stop conditions.

Activities

  1. Define the work boundary Name the outcome, affected parties, workflow boundary, and decision context.
  2. Assess consequence dimensions Evaluate judgment, determinism, data sensitivity, affected parties, reversibility, and authority.
  3. Select the control posture Choose the consequence level and map it to permissions, validation, authority, monitoring, and stop conditions.
  4. Accept the classification Record the accepting authority, evidence basis, next action, and reassessment trigger.
