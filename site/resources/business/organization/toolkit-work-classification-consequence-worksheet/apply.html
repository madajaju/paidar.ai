How to use this stage

Translate the selected consequence level into a control posture that another reviewer can apply.

  • Assign the accountable owner and required validator.
  • Define source, data, review, approval, and escalation requirements.
  • Record what must happen when required evidence or authority is missing.

Your result

What you should have after this stage

  • A completed work classification
  • A documented consequence rationale
  • A selected control posture with validation and authority requirements
  • Named ownership, evidence, stop conditions, and next review date
Suggested sequence

Put the guidance into practice

  1. Define the work boundary Name the outcome, affected parties, workflow boundary, and decision context.
  2. Assess consequence dimensions Evaluate judgment, determinism, data sensitivity, affected parties, reversibility, and authority.
  3. Select the control posture Choose the consequence level and map it to permissions, validation, authority, monitoring, and stop conditions.
  4. Accept the classification Record the accepting authority, evidence basis, next action, and reassessment trigger.
