The need

Why use this asset?

AI can produce a polished answer without making its evidence, assumptions, authority, or remaining risk visible. The Integrity Packet gives a qualified reviewer the record needed to evaluate and safely use the work.

Good uses for this asset

  • Compare purchasing bids while preserving source citations, evaluation criteria, and approval authority.
  • Prepare a finance variance explanation while separating verified figures from assumptions.
  • Draft an HR policy or candidate communication while protecting personal data and preserving human review.
  • Summarize administrative records or prepare a recommendation with a clear evidence trail.

Example uses

Purchasing comparison
Use AI to compare bids while retaining source citations, evaluation criteria, assumptions, and approval authority for review.

Finance variance explanation
Separate verified figures from assumptions and record the checks a qualified reviewer must complete before release.

HR or administrative communication
Draft a communication from bounded source material while protecting personal data and preserving human ownership of the final decision.

How it works

Work through the offering

What you bring

  • one purchasing, finance, HR, or administrative workflow
  • the intended outcome and consequence level
  • named source material and applicable constraints
  • a qualified reviewer or peer for the transfer test

What you leave with

  • draft Integrity Packet
  • structured prompt with sources, constraints, review, and stop conditions
  • validated AI-assisted output with visible assumptions and evidence
  • named owner, approver, unresolved risk, and escalation route
  • one workflow or governance action to adopt

Activities

  1. Define the workflow and consequence Choose a real or provided administrative workflow, state the intended outcome, and identify the consequence level and authority boundary.
  2. Draft a bounded prompt Separate the task, outcome, named sources, constraints, required evidence, human review, and stop condition.
  3. Complete the Integrity Packet Record the outcome, visible assumptions, evidence and traceability, validation status, ownership, and risk or escalation.
  4. Run the transfer test Have a reviewer assess what was produced, why it should be trusted, what was checked, and what remains unresolved without hidden author context.

Completion criteria

  • workflow outcome and consequence level recorded
  • sources, constraints, assumptions, and AI role made visible
  • important claims validated against the source record
  • Prepared By, Validated By, and Approved By identified
  • unresolved risk, owner, and escalation route recorded
  • reviewer can understand and act using the packet without hidden context
