Stage objective

Understand the six fields that make AI-assisted work transferable and defensible.

This page is part of Becoming AI-Augmented - The Integrity Packet, a resource for individual working in ai-augmented-organizations.

How to use this stage

Learn why a polished answer is not enough and how evidence, assumptions, validation, ownership, and risk support a safe handoff.

  • Describe the outcome and consequence level.
  • Make assumptions and missing evidence visible.
  • Record evidence and traceability for important claims.
  • Identify who validates, approves, owns, and escalates the result.

What you will produce

  • A draft Integrity Packet for one consequential workflow.
  • A bounded prompt that names sources, constraints, review, and stop conditions.
  • A governance action for the workflow owner, approver, or escalation path.

Parent asset:
A 45 minute IUC breakout where participants use workflows, prompting strategies, and governance practices to produce AI assisted work that is accurate, transparent, and defensible.

Your result

What you should have after this stage

  • A draft Integrity Packet for one consequential workflow.
  • A bounded prompt that names sources, constraints, review, and stop conditions.
  • A governance action for the workflow owner, approver, or escalation path.
Suggested sequence

Put the guidance into practice

  1. Define the workflow and consequence Choose a real or provided administrative workflow, state the intended outcome, and identify the consequence level and authority boundary.
  2. Draft a bounded prompt Separate the task, outcome, named sources, constraints, required evidence, human review, and stop condition.
  3. Complete the Integrity Packet Record the outcome, visible assumptions, evidence and traceability, validation status, ownership, and risk or escalation.
  4. Run the transfer test Have a reviewer assess what was produced, why it should be trusted, what was checked, and what remains unresolved without hidden author context.

When this stage is complete

  • workflow outcome and consequence level recorded
  • sources, constraints, assumptions, and AI role made visible
  • important claims validated against the source record
  • Prepared By, Validated By, and Approved By identified
  • unresolved risk, owner, and escalation route recorded
  • reviewer can understand and act using the packet without hidden context
