# Diagnose Posture Assessment

Score each item 1 to 5.

1. I know when AI should not be used.
2. I can explain how an AI-assisted output was produced.
3. I can identify the source material behind important claims.
4. I validate output before sharing it.
5. I document assumptions and uncertainty.
6. I reuse workflows instead of starting from scratch.
7. I can tell polished writing from defensible reasoning.
8. I know who owns final accountability.
9. I track rework caused by AI-assisted output.
10. I improve the system after failures.

## Interpretation

- 10-22: Under-leveraged or unmanaged.
- 23-34: Faking fluency risk.
- 35-43: Grounded but slow.
- 44-50: Augmented architect pattern.
