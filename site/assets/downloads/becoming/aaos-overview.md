# AI-Augmented Operating Standard Overview

AAOS turns AI-assisted work into defensible, repeatable operating practice.

## Standard Moves

1. Diagnose posture and risk.
2. Activate balanced capability.
3. Control speed through grounding and validation.
4. Execute reusable workflow loops.
5. Measure outcome quality, not just time saved.
6. Scale judgment through standards and handoffs.

## Minimum Reliable Artifact

Every consequential AI-assisted output should identify:

- Outcome
- Assumptions
- Evidence
- Validation
- Ownership
- Risk
