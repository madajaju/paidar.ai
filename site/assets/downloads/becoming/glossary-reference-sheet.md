# Becoming AI-Augmented Glossary and Reference Sheet

## Key Terms

**AI-Augmented Operating Standard:** A practical standard for making AI-assisted work reliable, grounded, and repeatable.

**Individual Integrity Packet:** A six-part artifact covering Outcome, Assumptions, Evidence, Validation, Ownership, and Risk.

**Verified Output Ratio:** The share of AI-assisted outputs that pass defined validation checks.

**Correction Load:** Human work required to make AI output usable.

**Grounding Compliance:** The degree to which outputs cite or reflect real source material and local truth.

**Workflow Loop:** A repeatable operating pattern with trigger, inputs, AI steps, human checks, validation, and metric.

**Silent Failure:** A failure mode where output looks polished but drifts from truth, intent, policy, or context.
