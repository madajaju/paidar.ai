Why Control Matters

Fast Output Is Not Enough

Consequential work needs grounding, validation, and ownership. Control makes the difference between a plausible output and a defensible professional artifact.

  • Ground work in source files, live data, constraints, exclusions, and manual checks.
  • Use a source log to separate AI-used, human-verified, and still-uncertain material.
  • Escalate when consequence, uncertainty, or exposure exceed the automation boundary.
