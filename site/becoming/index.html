Resources for the Six-Part AAOS Journey

These companion pages organize the QR resources from Becoming AI-Augmented around the practical journey: Diagnose, Activate, Control, Execute, Measure, and Scale. The files are designed to be printed, copied into your own workspace, or adapted as operating artifacts.

Resources may be updated after publication. Keep this page bookmarked as the stable hub for errata, new templates, and version notes.
