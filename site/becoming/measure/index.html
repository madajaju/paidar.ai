Core Metrics

Signals Worth Tracking

Verified Output Ratio

Outputs that pass checks divided by outputs produced.

Correction Load

Human effort required to make AI-assisted work usable.

Grounding Compliance

Percent of outputs tied to evidence, sources, or local truth.

Insight Conversion

How often AI-assisted insight becomes a real system change.

Rework Rate

How often output returns for correction after review.

Reclaimed Time ROI

Time saved minus correction, validation, and coordination cost.
