Book 4

Educating the AI-Augmented

Championing Real Learning in the Age of AI.

What It Is

A fundamental shift in educational strategy that moves from grading "Black Box" products to grading verifiable "Learning Journeys."

Who It's For

K-12 educators, higher education faculty, and academic leaders who are struggling with AI integrity and engagement.

What You'll Learn

  • The Posture Matrix: Mapping how students actually use AI
  • How to design assignments that AI cannot easily "fake"
  • Strategies for turning AI from a shortcut into a reasoning tool
  • The "Glass Box" classroom: grading the process, not just the result

Outcome

Educators regain confidence in assessment integrity while students build deep domain expertise alongside AI capability.
